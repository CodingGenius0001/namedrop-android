DapTapSource

NameDrop for Android. Hold your phone to an iPhone and it plays its own NameDrop: your cards swap, and they need no app.

  1. 1
    Make your cardName, number, photo, and a colour that's yours.
  2. 2
    Set up the iPhone link onceDapTap walks you through it in a few taps, and keeps it running after that.
  3. 3
    Touch phones, back to backThe iPhone shows its NameDrop card. When they tap Share, their card lands in DapTap and yours on their iPhone, ready to save. Another DapTap phone swaps instantly.
Download the APK How it works

Made for the Galaxy S26 on One UI 9 (Android 17), which can raise the Wi-Fi link iPhones use for NameDrop. Samsung's Auto Blocker has to be off, because the iPhone link runs through Wireless debugging.
